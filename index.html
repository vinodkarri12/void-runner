KEY ACTION DESCRIPTION

A D / ← → Steer Steer left / right across stone paths and bridges
SHIFT / B SHADOW BOOST Hold while running for shadow speed surge (consumes boost meter)
J / SPACE Katana Attack Normal attack, rising cross-slash combo, and leap finisher
K Jump Acrobatic leap over obstacles and surrounding enemies
CTRL Slide Low shadow slide under barriers while preserving momentum
E Dash Attack Rapid target-seeking forward dash into an Iaijutsu Katana strike
Q Reaper Strike Quick Katana cut / Shuriken ranged strike
F Katana Slash Wide sweeping high-damage Katana slash
HOLD R Iaijutsu Blast Focus energy, release for destructive area blast
T Overdrive Surge damage and max velocity
G / V / X / Z Shinobi Jutsu Shadow Clone / chain grapple / anchor / execution finisher
MOUSE / C Camera / Reset Orbit third-person camera or recenter behind ninja
ESC / P Pause Open or close tactical pause console

Q / F Katana Strikes Reaper strike and wide high-damage Katana slash
HOLD R Iaijutsu Charge inner energy, release to detonate
